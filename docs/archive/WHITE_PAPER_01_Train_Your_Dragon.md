## Abstract

Most AI interaction resets context, concentrates durable state in the platform, and treats the human as a temporary user. Train Your Dragon is a live-tested alternative: the human builds and owns durable external systems (structured memory, skills, process rules) so existing AI capabilities compound under human direction.

This paper documents the working model, its principles, development history, and relation to digital minimalism and AI-second-brain systems. It does not claim to solve global AI governance. It claims that **one human can keep agency** in daily AI collaboration by design — and that this personal node is the necessary building block for any larger network that still respects ownership.
